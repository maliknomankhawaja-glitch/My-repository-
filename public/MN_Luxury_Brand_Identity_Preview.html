Section 06 · Sartorial Rigor

Crafted with Precision — The 6 Anatomy Pillars

Every internal component is specified according to authentic bespoke tailoring standards.

Pillar 01
Clean AMF Pick Stitching

1.5mm pick stitching secures the floating canvas to the wool shell and creates subtle dimensional roll along the lapel edge.

Pillar 02
Full Floating Canvas

Zero heat-fusing glue. Multi-layer horsehair and camel felt chest canvas hand-padded for living breathability and lifetime drape.

Pillar 03
Genuine Buffalo Horn Buttons

Laser-etched with M.N monogram and hand-attached with wrapped thread shanks for functional longevity.

Pillar 04
Savile Row Structured Silhouette

Lightly roped pagoda shoulders and mild chest suppression sculpt a commanding athletic posture.

Pillar 05
Breathable Bemberg Cupro Lining

Pure cotton linter cupro with tone-on-tone jacquard M.N monogram prevents friction and moisture buildup.

Pillar 06
Forward-Pleated Dress Trousers

Double forward pleats, solid brass side buckle adjusters, and unfinished hems for exact tailor break.
